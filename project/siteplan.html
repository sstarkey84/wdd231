California Hunting Guide Site Plan

Site Name

California Hunting Guide

I chose the name California Hunting Guide because it clearly describes the purpose of the website. The site will provide information to help hunters find hunting seasons, game species, zones, and other hunting information in California.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the California Hunting Guide is to provide hunters with information about California game species, hunting seasons, hunting zones, and license or tag requirements. The site will help users find and compare hunting opportunities and prepare for a hunt in California.

Scenarios

  • What game species can I hunt in California and when are their hunting seasons?
  • Which hunting zones and tag requirements apply to the species I want to hunt?
  • How can I compare hunting opportunities for different species in California?

Color Scheme

The color scheme uses natural outdoor colors while maintaining strong contrast for readability and accessibility.

  • Dark Forest Green (#1F3A2E):Used for the header, footer, headings, and major accents with white text.
  • Dark Brown (#493628):Used for secondary headings, borders, and accent elements.
  • Light Cream (#FAF7F0):Used for the main page background with dark text for readability.

Typography

Arial will be used throughout the website because it is simple, readable, and works well on different screen sizes. Bold Arial will be used for headings, and regular Arial will be used for paragraphs, lists, and other body text.
